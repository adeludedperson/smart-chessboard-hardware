Dry-fit rules

  1. Resistor legs need bending: bend both wires down 90° so the bends are ~10mm apart, then drop in.
  2. IC legs need a squeeze: DIP legs splay outward from the factory. Press each row gently against the table to angle them straight, then insert. Match the chip's notch to the notch drawn on the board.
  3. Sensors: TEXT side of the package faces the FLAT side of the printed outline (yes — that's backwards vs normal; your clones are mirror-pinout). Bottom side of the board.
  4. Never force. Everything drops in with light pressure. If it doesn't, wrong part or wrong spot.
  5. When done, compare against render_top_50mm.png / render_bottom_50mm.png — they show the finished board.
