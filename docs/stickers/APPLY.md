# Decal — order & apply

## What to tell the print shop

- File: one of the `decal_*.pdf` files (pick color at the counter if undecided).
- Print size: **exactly 440 × 440 mm** — tell them "print at 100%, do not
  scale to fit". Ask them to verify a square measures 50 mm after printing.
- Material: **self-adhesive vinyl (eco-solvent print) + matte lamination**.
- Cut: straight cut on the artwork edge (440 × 440).

## Alignment geometry (why 45 mm matters)

The 4 corner sensors of the array sit 25 mm inside the playing area, and the
playing area has a 20 mm border → each **corner sensor centre is exactly
45 mm diagonally in from the decal's corner** (45 mm from each edge).

## Apply (30–40 min, do it dry-run first)

1. Clean the acrylic top with isopropyl/glass cleaner. No dust, no oil.
2. With the acrylic on the board, mark all **4 corner sensor centres** on the
   acrylic with a fine marker (visible through it).
3. From each mark, measure 45 mm out toward the corner in both x and y and
   draw a short L-shaped guide — these 4 Ls are where the decal corners land.
   If the Ls disagree by a millimetre (board gaps), centre between them —
   split the difference.
4. Spray the acrylic with water + one drop of dish soap.
5. Peel the decal, lay it down inside the L-guides. It floats — slide it
   until all 4 corners sit on the guides.
6. Squeegee from centre outward (credit card wrapped in cloth works).
   Push all water out. Wipe edges.
7. Leave 24 h before heavy use; adhesive cures as the water evaporates.

Marker lines outside the decal wipe off with isopropyl afterwards.
