# V2 renders — the acrylic-top board

The board is 4 flat layers stacked like a sandwich. Nothing is drilled. Light from
underneath shines straight through the milky top sheet.

## v2_assembled.png
The finished board from above and to the side. Two squares glow soft green —
that's the LEDs under the surface showing you a move.

## v2_exploded.png
The same board with the layers lifted apart so you can see the stack. Top to
bottom: white acrylic sheet → green boards holding the sensors and lights →
hollow gap holding the brains → wooden base. The sliced pawn on the left shows
the magnet buried in its base.

## v2_front_edge.png
Close-up of the front edge. The small screen and 3 buttons sit in the wooden
rim, poking out through the gap between the top sheet and the base.

These are concept renders of an LED + acrylic variant that was not built.
The Blender sources are not in this repository.
